## The original game

Darkwood is a shareware game for the classic Macintosh written by **Robert Chancellor** and
released in 1992 (version 1.2 is the one taken apart here). Its pictures, icons, sounds, map,
text, the manual (`notes/ReadMe.txt`) and the program itself (`original/`, `extracted/`) are his
work and remain his copyright. They are included here for preservation and for this
non-commercial remake, in the same spirit as the archives that have kept the game available
since the 1990s. The original asked for a $10 registration; if you enjoy this, that is still
the right gesture, should the author be reachable.

Nothing in this repository claims any right to that material, and none of it is covered by the
MIT license below. If you are the rights holder and want any of it removed, open an issue or
contact the repository owner and it will be taken down.

Another remake, _Siege of Darkwood_ by petpolitics (PICO-8, 2020), credits the same original.
